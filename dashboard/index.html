Empirical Research Findings

Saved evaluation results over the 75 hand-labelled question benchmark. All numbers are verified from saved experiment artifacts.

Key Findings Summary

  • Retrieval Ranking: Hybrid RRF achieves the highest Recall@5 (0.9478) and nDCG@10 (0.8461) on the 67 answerable questions, outperforming BM25 (0.9179) and Dense (0.9254) while maintaining fast warm queries (18.8 ms).
  • Routing Accuracy: The lightweight pre-retrieval heuristic correctly routes 72/75 questions (96.0%) with a Balanced Accuracy of 0.9225 and F1 of 0.9774, far exceeding fixed baselines (Balanced Accuracy = 0.5000). It avoids unnecessary retrieval for 7 of 8 unanswerable questions.
  • Efficiency Trade-Off: Retrieval does not make answering faster or cheaper — retrieval adds sub-millisecond overhead (< 0.5 ms), but grounded generation takes ~7.2 seconds longer (warm) and uses ~4.4× more tokens due to evidence context. Adaptive retrieval preserves answer quality while avoiding this overhead for questions that do not need documents.
